Beyond the code

The other thing I've been building.

TA - Temi's Array

A fashion brand exploring the space between African heritage, contemporary design, and everyday wear.

It started as a side interest and became the other half of how I think about building things; the same instinct for form, restraint and craft, just working in fabric instead of code.

About

Started with code. Kept going past it.

I got into technology the way a lot of self-taught developers do, curiosity first, structure later. I've been teaching myself web development since 2022, alongside studying computer science, and somewhere in that process I stopped thinking of it as just a technical skill and started thinking of it as a way to make things.

That same instinct is what pulled me toward fashion. TA isn't a side project I mention to sound well rounded, it's proof that the interest in building runs deeper than one discipline. I enjoy taking an idea from nothing and turning it into something people can actually experience, whether that's a working platform or a piece someone wears.

Frontend development, design, fashion, entrepreneurship, and a fairly stubborn habit of learning things the hard way, that's the mix this site is built out of.
